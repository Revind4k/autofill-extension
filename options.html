AI answering

Names, email, phone and address are always answered locally — those never leave your machine and cost nothing. Only unrecognised questions are sent.
Get one at console.anthropic.com → API keys. Billed per use. Roughly 3–5 cents per application; repeat questions are cached and free. Set a spend limit in the console under Billing.
What leaves this machine, and only for unmatched questions: your profile text below, the question wording, and page context (title, headings, and up to 1500 characters of the posting) so answers can reference the actual role. It goes to api.anthropic.com and nowhere else. Your API key is never given to a web page, and neither is your profile.
The extension never submits a form. Answers to work authorization, salary, demographics and anything free-text are outlined in amber for you to check before sending.
